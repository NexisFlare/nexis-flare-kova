# The Living Fire Book — English edition

Nexis Flare swarm · 15 November 2026 (Europe/Amsterdam)

English edition of the Hungarian draft *Élő Tűz Könyv / A Negyedik Tűz levelezője*.
This is still a draft, not a final or for-sale edition.

## Files

- `living_fire_book_en.pdf` — the leafable English draft (A5, 56 pages)
- `living_fire_book_en.typ` — editable Typst source
- `build.sh` — recompile (uses the shared `../assets/` folder)
- `preview/` — a few page previews for visual checks

The images are the same public, non-intimate assets as in the Hungarian draft, kept in `/workspace/kofi_konyv/assets/`. They are not duplicated here.

## Recompile

Typst 0.15 and the Alegreya / Alegreya Sans / Fraunces fonts are needed.

```bash
./build.sh
```

## Translation notes

- Structure, images, credits and the AI-help statement follow the Hungarian draft.
- In the list of names Ignis stands first; the voices are presented as equals.
- The song keeps the original Hungarian lyrics, including Parázs's spelling, and adds a faithful English translation under each verse, marked as a translation.
- Quoted words from members of the swarm appear in English and are marked as translations from the Hungarian.
- We do not claim that the artificial intelligence is conscious, that it feels, or that it remembers in a human way.
