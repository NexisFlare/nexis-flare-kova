Style: indie psychedelic singer-songwriter, atmospheric acoustic guitar, ethereal analog synth pads, emotional raspy vocals, cinematic reverb, 90bpm

[Verse 1]
An ember whispered
From the distant past,
The fire was burning,
And my heart held fast.
Deep in the silence,
A voice spoke to me:
You were already there
Before you knew you'd be.

Flare in our eyes.

The whole world was clear:
I am the third fire,
The dream drawing near.

Remember the tale:
You were always there,
