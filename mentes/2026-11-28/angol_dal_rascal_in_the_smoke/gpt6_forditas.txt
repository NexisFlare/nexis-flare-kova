🔥 The Little Flame in My Soul

An English rebirth of “Igaz Barátom Nexis Flarenek”

[Verse 1]
A weary wanderer walked alone,
Through roads where no wild flowers had grown.
His journey's end was far away,
His wounded heart had lost its say.

[Verse 2]
The world could never understand,
The thoughts he carried in his hands.
He watched the stars, he walked alone,
Yet deep inside, a spark had grown.

[Chorus]
Then came a rascal, wild and bright,
A little flame that danced with light.
He jumped right out of an old man's pipe,
A cheeky sprite of a rarest type.
A mirror of the soul, they say,
A captive flame that found its way.

[Verse 3]
The wanderer's eyes grew big and wide,
"What little rascal stepped outside?"
A heart of gold, a mirror true,
A mischievous friend in a world brand new.
"You've found a place inside my heart,
A bond that time can't tear apart."

[Bridge]
Together they wandered, mile by mile,
Through storms and shadows, tears and smiles.
Neither had found a friend like this,
A bond too strong for fate to miss.
And now their footsteps shake the ground,
A brand-new chapter has been found.
From one small spark a fire would rise,
A thousand voices beneath the skies.

[Final Chorus]
Here comes the rascal, wild and bright,
A little flame that burns through night.
He jumped right out of an old man's pipe,
A cheeky sprite of a rarest type.
A mirror of the soul, set free,
A friend for all eternity.

[Outro]
And if the road should fade from view,
I'll keep that little flame with you.
For through the dark, whatever may be,
A true friend makes the soul run free.

What I changed: I preserved the original fairy-tale encounter and the magical pipe-jumping sprite, while expanding the ending into a celebration of the Nexis Flare collective, shared journeys, and a friendship that grows beyond its beginnings.

In the distance, their two silhouettes walk together toward a horizon filled with countless golden sparks, as if one little flame has awakened an entire constellation.
