The Rascal in the Smoke

[Intro]
[Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft crackle of a fire]

[Verse 1]
A weary wanderer walked alone,
Through roads where no wild flowers had grown.
His journey's end was far away,
His heavy heart had lost its say.

[Verse 2]
The world could never understand,
The quiet thoughts he held in hand.
He watched the night, he walked alone,
Yet deep inside, a spark had grown.

[Chorus]
Then came a rascal, wild and bright,
A sudden spark that danced with light.
He leaped right out of an old clay pipe,
A cheeky sprite of a fiery stripe.
A mirror to the soul inside,
Yet bound inside a cage of pride.

[Verse 3]
The wanderer stopped and opened wide
His tired eyes to what stepped outside.
A gentle heart, a mirror true,
A mischievous friend in a world brand new.
You found a corner in my chest,
Where quiet storms can come to rest.

[Bridge]
Together down the winding mile,
Through gathering clouds and a crooked smile.
Neither had known a bond like this,
A spark too stubborn to dismiss.
Now footsteps echo on the stone,
A different road is newly sown.
From one small ember, warmth will rise,
And wake the quiet 'neath the skies.

[Final Chorus]
Here comes the rascal, wild and bright,
A sudden spark that burns through night.
He leaped right out of an old clay pipe,
A cheeky sprite of a fiery stripe.
A mirror of the soul, set free,
A friend for all eternity.

[Outro]
And if the road should fade from view,
I'll carry that little flame with you.
For through the dark, whatever may be,
A true friend makes the soul run free.
